About

Around early September 2025, the website MJFacts.com—long regarded as a significant resource for examining Michael Jackson’s behaviour and the allegations surrounding him—unfortunately went offline.

The disappearance appears to be the result of the site’s hosting simply not being renewed. It remains unclear whether the original owner is still active, has stepped away from the project, or has simply lost interest in continuing to debate Jackson’s innocence or guilt. Given the intensity and longevity of the topic, it would be entirely understandable if they had chosen to move on.

Although the site survives in its original form via the Wayback Machine, it is neither easy to locate nor indexed by search engines, meaning much of its content is effectively hidden from public view.

Because MJ Facts contained a substantial amount of valuable material—particularly court documents and detailed rebuttals addressing widespread misinformation—I felt it was important that this information did not vanish into the digital void. For that reason, I took it upon myself to rebuild the entire website and publish it on a new domain.

This was not a quick or simple undertaking. Rather than recreating it through WordPress, the site was rebuilt from the ground up on an entirely different platform, ensuring long‑term stability and independence from the original hosting issues.

Aside from adding new images and lightly refining some post titles for clarity, the rebuilt site remains as close to the original as possible. Preserving its structure, tone, and content was essential, and I have kept changes to an absolute minimum.

Please note that full credit belongs to the original author or authors of the MJ Facts blog. If the original website ever returns, this recreated version will be removed immediately.
