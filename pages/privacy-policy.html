Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MJ Facts collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

MJ Facts is built using static HTML and does not use cookies, trackers, analytics scripts, or any other form of behavioural monitoring. There are no cookie banners, no pop‑ups, and no hidden consent mechanisms because we do not collect or store personal browsing data. The only information we record is the number of visitors to the site, which helps us understand general traffic patterns without identifying individual users.

Some pages on MJ Facts may include embedded content from third‑party platforms such as YouTube, Twitter, or other social media services. These external providers may place their own cookies or tracking technologies when their content loads. Because these tools originate from outside our website, we do not control how they operate or what data they collect.

Our site also contains numerous links to external websites that offer additional context, source material, or relevant commentary. Once you leave MJ Facts and visit a third‑party site, their own privacy policies and practices apply. If you have concerns about how these platforms use cookies or track user behaviour, we encourage you to review their published privacy policies directly.

If you prefer to block third‑party cookies entirely, you may wish to use privacy‑focused browser extensions such as Privacy Badger, Ghostery, or uBlock Origin. These tools can help you manage what loads in your browser and limit unwanted tracking.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Privacy expectations evolve, and so does the technology that powers the web. For this reason, MJ Facts may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will reflect adjustments to our practices, legal obligations, or the tools used on the website.

If we ever introduce essential cookies, analytics tools, or any form of visitor‑tracking technology, this policy will be updated to explain precisely what is being collected, why it is necessary, and how it affects your data. Significant changes may take effect immediately, although we will make reasonable efforts to notify users when major updates occur.
