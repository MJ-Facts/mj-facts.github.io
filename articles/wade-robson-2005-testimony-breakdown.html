Wade Robson’s Testimony from the 2005 Michael Jackson Molestation Trial

15 May, 2013

  1. Categories
  2. Robson Case

Wade Robson’s 2005 testimony in the Michael Jackson molestation trial has become one of the most scrutinised moments in the case’s history. Although he firmly denied any wrongdoing at the time, observers noted how visibly tense he appeared on the stand and how uncertain many of his answers seemed. In hindsight, those reactions have taken on new significance. When Robson came forward in 2013 describing years of grooming and sexual abuse, his earlier demeanour and inconsistencies began to look less like confidence and more like the conflicted behaviour of someone still deeply entangled in the psychological grip of a powerful abuser.

Note: Wade Robson’s specific denials of molestation and inappropriate behaviour are highlighted in this color.
